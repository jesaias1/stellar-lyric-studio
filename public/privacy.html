Stellar Lyric Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Stellar Lyric Studio ("SLS", "the studio") is a tool made by Stellar Music, Copenhagen, Denmark, for making lyric videos and posting them to the social media accounts of the artists and profiles it works with. This page explains what the studio does with your data.

Who is responsible

Stellar Music, Copenhagen, Denmark, is the data controller. Contact us through stellarmusic.dk.

What the studio handles

  • Songs and lyrics. When you add a song, the studio sends it in short pieces to an AI speech model run by Cloudflare (Workers AI) so the lyrics can be written and timed. The pieces are used for that and are not stored.
  • Your account. You log in with a username and the team code. The studio keeps your username and the time the account was made, in a database run by Supabase.
  • Projects. Songs, photos and clips stay in your own browser (IndexedDB) on your device. The edit itself (lyrics, timings, style, cuts and texts) is also saved to your account, so you can open it on another device.
  • Footage and photo searches. Search words and lyrics are sent to the image and video services you choose (Pexels, Pixabay, SerpApi for Pinterest results, Higgsfield for AI backgrounds) to find or make footage.
  • Linked accounts. When you link a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account, that platform gives the studio a login (an access token) and basic profile details: the account name, ID and profile picture. The login is encrypted by the studio's server and saved to your studio account. Only the studio's server can decrypt it, and only to post on your instruction. Nobody else logged in to the studio can use it.
  • Videos you post. A video is sent through the studio's server to the platforms you pick. For Instagram, which collects videos by link, the video is kept in the studio's file storage (Vercel Blob) for at most 3 hours, then deleted. A post you schedule for later is kept there until it has been posted, then deleted.
  • Scheduled posts and results. For posts you schedule or make, the studio saves the account, time, caption, the link to the post and its results (views, likes, comments) to your account, so you can see them in the calendar. It reads those results from the platforms using the same linked login.
  • Hosting. The studio runs on Vercel. The lyric AI runs on Cloudflare Workers AI.

What the studio asks the platforms for

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and read the channel's name. The studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to read basic account details and publish media.
  • TikTok: permission to read basic account details and to send videos to your drafts or post them.

The studio does not read your messages, followers, analytics or other content, and does not sell or share your data for advertising.

How long data is kept

Linked account logins stay saved to your studio account until you remove the account in the studio or the platform's login expires. Projects stay until you delete them. Posted videos are held for at most 3 hours (Instagram only), and scheduled ones until they are posted. Server logs kept for troubleshooting are deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

To delete what the studio holds about you:

  1. Remove a linked YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account in the studio's posting window. That deletes its saved login at once. You can also remove the studio in the platform's own settings (Instagram: Settings, Apps and websites; TikTok: Settings, Security, Manage app permissions; Google: account permissions).
  2. Delete projects from the studio's start screen.
  3. To delete your whole studio account and everything saved to it, contact Stellar Music through stellarmusic.dk with your username. It is deleted within 30 days, and you get a confirmation.

Your choices and rights

  • Remove a linked account in the studio's posting window at any time.
  • Cut off the studio's access completely in each platform's settings: Google account permissions, Instagram's Apps and websites settings, and TikTok's Manage app permissions.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes
