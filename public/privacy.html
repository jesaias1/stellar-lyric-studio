SLS Stellar Lyric Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Stellar Lyric Studio ("SLS", "the studio") is a tool made by Stellar Music, Copenhagen, Denmark, for making lyric videos and posting them to the social media accounts of the artists and profiles it works with. This page explains what the studio does with your data.

Who is responsible

Stellar Music, Copenhagen, Denmark, is the data controller. Contact us through stellarmusic.dk.

What the studio handles

  • Songs and lyrics. When you add a song, the studio sends it in short pieces to an AI speech model run by Cloudflare (Workers AI) so the lyrics can be written and timed. The pieces are used for that and are not stored.
  • Projects. Your projects, songs, photos and clips are saved in your own browser (IndexedDB) and stay on your device.
  • Footage and photo searches. Search words and lyrics are sent to the image and video services you choose (Pexels, Pixabay, SerpApi for Pinterest results, Higgsfield for AI backgrounds) to find or make footage.
  • Linked accounts. When you link a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account, that platform gives the studio a login (an access token) and basic profile details: the account name, ID and profile picture. The login is encrypted and kept in the browser that linked the account. Only the studio's server can decrypt it, and only to post on your instruction.
  • Videos you post. A video is sent through the studio's server to the platforms you pick. For Instagram, which collects videos by link, the video is kept on the server for at most 3 hours, then deleted.

What the studio asks the platforms for

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and read the channel's name. The studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to read basic account details and publish media.
  • TikTok: permission to read basic account details and to send videos to your drafts or post them.

The studio does not read your messages, followers, analytics or other content, and does not sell or share your data for advertising.

How long data is kept

Linked account logins stay in your browser until you remove the account in the studio, clear your browser data, or the platform's login expires. Posted videos are held on the server for at most 3 hours (Instagram only). Server logs kept by Cloudflare for troubleshooting are deleted automatically.

Your choices and rights

  • Remove a linked account in the studio's posting window at any time.
  • Cut off the studio's access completely in each platform's settings: Google account permissions, Instagram's Apps and websites settings, and TikTok's Manage app permissions.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
